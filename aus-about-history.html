Where It All Began

The Founding Story

The story of the AUS Business School began in 1963, at the creation of the American College of Switzerland, whose academic body had the vision of combining both the effectiveness of American graduate education with the Swiss work ethic.

Building upon this vision, four of the ACS teachers created the American Graduate School of Business in 1991, which flourished into one of the leading international business schools in Switzerland.

In 2019, the school changed its name from the American Graduate School of Business to the AUS Business School to reflect its growing international education status.

Milestones

History Through the Years

1991

AGSB founded

Four ACS teachers create the American Graduate School of Business in La Tour-de-Peilz, Switzerland.

1992

Campus established

AGSB sets up its permanent campus in La Tour-de-Peilz, welcoming its first cohorts.

1993

AVDEP recognition

Recognized by the Association of Private Schools in Canton Vaud.

1994

SFPS membership

Joins the Swiss Federation of Private Schools.

1996

ECIS membership

Becomes a member of the European Council of International Schools.

1998

College Board & VA approval

Gains College Board membership and approval by the U.S. Department of Veteran Affairs.

2013

AAHEA accreditation

Accredited by the American Association for Higher Education.

2014

ASIC accreditation

Gains Accreditation Service for International Schools, Colleges & Universities.

2016

New campus inauguration

Opens the doors to its expanded, modern campus in La Tour-de-Peilz.

2017

Global partnerships

Signs academic agreements with institutions in Mexico, Colombia, Georgia, Lebanon, and India. Reaccredited by IACBE.

2018

ECBE & expansion

Reaccredited by ECBE. Partnerships with institutions in Saudi Arabia. Establishment of AGSB S.A.

2019

AUS is born

Renamed to AUS Business School. IATA certification. Dual degree partnership with Tiffin University. New partnerships in Malaysia, Jordan, and Saudi Arabia.

2020

29th anniversary

AUS celebrates nearly three decades of academic excellence and international education.
