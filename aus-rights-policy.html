Policies and Procedures

Institutional policies, data protection, and privacy at AUS.

The American Institute of Applied Sciences in Switzerland has developed and maintains a system of policies aimed at ensuring a clear, transparent and predictable experience, enhancing the quality of the student learning journey. Policy management at AUS is based on the principle of institution-wide participation in the formulation of policies, as well as timely implementation of policies and monitoring thereof.
