Academic Excellence

Meet Our Faculty

Our faculty brings together distinguished academics and seasoned industry professionals from around the world. With diverse backgrounds in business, finance, marketing, and leadership, they deliver a learning experience that bridges theory and practice.

Every instructor at AUS is committed to small-class engagement, mentorship, and preparing students for real-world challenges in international business.
