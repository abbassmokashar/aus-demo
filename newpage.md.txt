# 18. SCHOLARSHIP CALCULATION

This is a critical rule.

The scholarship applies ONLY to tuition.

Correct:

```text
Scholarship Amount =
Original Tuition × Scholarship %
```

Then:

```text
Net Tuition =
Original Tuition − Scholarship Amount
```

Example:

Original tuition:
CHF 30,000

20% scholarship:
CHF 6,000

Net tuition:
CHF 24,000

These numbers are an illustration only.

Do not hard-code them.

---

# 19. SCHOLARSHIP MUST NOT REDUCE LIVING COSTS

NEVER calculate:

```text
(total tuition + living expenses) × scholarship
```

That is incorrect.

The correct structure is:

```text
Net Tuition
+
Living Costs
+
Visa
+
Permit
+
Applicable Fees
=
Estimated Total
```

Scholarship must never reduce:

* accommodation
* food
* health insurance
* mobile
* transportation
* visa
* student permit
* administrative fees
