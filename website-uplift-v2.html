MISSION

AUS equips students with the knowledge, skills, and tools to contribute to the development and success of organizations. Through practice-oriented studies based on the application of scientific methods, research and development, AUS graduates are prepared to become competent and confident leaders.

VISION

Our vision is to build a reputation as a prestigious and well-known private higher education institution in Switzerland that attracts students and faculty from all over the world.

Practice-led, not theory-
bound.

Every course is built around real organizational problems. Students leave with a portfolio of decisions they made, not just a transcript of lectures they attended, prepared to lead from day one rather than catch up.
