06 Mission & Vision
Mission

AUS equips students pursuing a degree in international business with the knowledge, skills and tools to make a significant contribution to any organization. Through practice-oriented study grounded in scientific method, research and development, graduates are prepared to lead with competence and confidence.

Vision

To build a reputation as a prestigious, well-known private institution in Switzerland that attracts students and faculty from all over the world, and to shape decision-makers ready for a more sustainable future.

07 Why AUS

Five reasons students choose AUS.

01

Join a genuinely international student body.

Classrooms bring together students from across Europe, the Middle East and beyond, so every group project is already a cross-border negotiation.

02

Case work replaces the lecture hall.

Assignments are built from real organizational problems, not textbook exercises, so the skills transfer directly to a first job.

03

The working language of international business.

Every program runs entirely in English, preparing graduates to operate in global teams from day one.

04

Study where students actually want to live.

The campus sits on the shores of Lake Geneva, close enough to Zurich and Lucerne to make the most of both.

05

Faculty who know your name, not your seat number.

Small cohorts mean direct access to professors, real feedback on your work, and no getting lost in a lecture hall of hundreds.

08 Sustainability

Shaping decision-makers for a sustainable future.

Sustainability sits at the core of how AUS teaches. Through partnerships with associations and non-profits, sustainable practice is built into the classroom, so graduates can balance economic and environmental goals in line with the UN Sustainable Development Goals.
