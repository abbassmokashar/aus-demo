Overview

A traditional accounting pathway, built for the modern workplace.

The Accounting specialization gives you a strong foundation in accounting principles, financial analysis, taxation, and business decision-making. It's built for students aiming at careers in accounting, finance, auditing, taxation, or business management — pairing technical knowledge with the practical problem-solving used by today's accounting professionals.

The double degree allows students enrolled in the program to earn a degree from Switzerland and a degree from the United States, while studying entirely in Switzerland.

Program Description

Academic excellence meets practical experience.

The BSc in Business Administration is designed to prepare you for the future of business through a mix of academic knowledge, practical experience, and a genuinely global perspective. You'll learn to pair traditional business fundamentals with decision-making, problem-solving, creativity, and strategic thinking.

"Courses are delivered in small, dynamic cohorts by experienced faculty with strong academic and professional backgrounds."

Many professors bring direct business experience into the classroom, so you learn from real challenges and current industry practice rather than theory alone — teaching here is student-centered and experiential from day one.

Dual Swiss & US Degree

Graduate with two degrees, from two continents.

+

The BSc in International Business Administration at AUS is offered exclusively as a dual degree with Tiffin University in the United States. Every enrolled student graduates with two degrees: a BSc in International Business Administration from AUS, and a BBA from Tiffin University.

AUS courses are taught on campus in Switzerland, while Tiffin University courses may be completed on campus or remotely — with the option of exchanges to either university under certain conditions.

Accredited Pathway

Recognized on both sides of the Atlantic.

Tiffin University is accredited by the Higher Learning Commission in the United States, supporting the international credibility and recognition of the dual-degree pathway — a stronger international profile for graduates entering competitive job markets across Europe, the US, and beyond.

Career Outcomes

Your Future in Accounting

The Accounting specialization prepares you for careers in accounting, finance, taxation, auditing, consulting, and business management — building the technical knowledge, analytical skills, and ethical judgment organizations rely on for measuring performance, managing risk, and making informed financial decisions.

Accountant

Prepare, analyze, and manage financial records for companies, organizations, or clients.

Auditor

Review financial statements, internal controls, and business processes for accuracy and compliance.

Tax Consultant

Support individuals and organizations with tax planning, research, compliance, and reporting.

Financial Analyst

Use accounting and financial data to evaluate performance, forecast trends, and support strategy.

Cost Accountant

Help organizations plan budgets, control costs, and improve operational efficiency.

Consulting Associate

Advise businesses on financial processes, reporting systems, and risk management.

Finance Associate

Support investment decisions, budgeting, financial planning, and capital management.

Accounting Manager

With experience, progress into roles overseeing accounting operations and internal controls.

Core Requirements

To qualify, applicants must have completed secondary education in their home country, earning a diploma that grants university-level eligibility. No GMAT or entrance exam is required — admission is based on a holistic review and a mandatory online interview.
